# CS Platform - How to Use It (CSM Quick Overview)

A one-page overview of how to work in the platform day to day. The guiding idea:
**you don't decide what to do next - the platform surfaces it, prioritised, and you
action it.** You are an orchestrator, not a data-gatherer.

---

## The 60-second version

1. **Open the Command Center.** It shows your portfolio at a glance: Total ARR, GRR,
   NDR, ARR at risk, expansion pipeline, upcoming renewals, and the accounts that need
   attention this week.
2. **Work the prioritised queue top-down.** Every task is ranked by urgency and tagged
   with one of the three mandates. Start at the top and work down.
3. **Open an account** to see its full 360 (health, signals, usage, renewal, contacts,
   onboarding, calls) and take the recommended action.
4. **Ask Jane** (the AI analyst, top-right) anything about your book or a specific
   account. She answers with the evidence and the next best action.

That's the whole loop. The platform originates the work; you orchestrate it.

---

## The three mandates (how every task is categorised)

- **Must Protect** - churn and risk. Act within 24 hours (e.g. ML churn > 70%, a sudden
  seat drop, a Day-15 payment risk).
- **Must Expand** - renewals and upgrades. Run the T-120/90/60/30 renewal cadence and
  follow expansion triggers (e.g. licence utilisation >= 85%).
- **Must Use** - adoption and onboarding. Unblock stalled onboarding, drive feature
  adoption, keep contact roles tagged.

## Your weekly rhythm (Operating Rhythm page)

- **Monday**: review the portfolio and the week's priorities.
- **Daily**: clear the Priority-1 defensive-risk queue first.
- **Weekly blocks**: renewals/expansion, then adoption/QBR.

## The account 360 - what to check

Health score + what's driving it, renewal countdown, live usage, the Rocket Lane
implementation board, the Executive Sponsor F2F log, Jiminny call summaries, and the
contact roles. **Tag the three required roles** (Executive Sponsor, Primary Champion /
Admin, Finance Contact) - renewal and onboarding tasks can't be closed until they are.

## Jane (your AI analyst)

Docked on every page. Ask things like "what's at risk in my book?", "prepare my meeting
with [account]", or "why is [account] flagged?". Jane is evidence-grounded (she won't
invent numbers), judged against the signed playbook, and read-only - she proposes, you
approve.

---

## How you make it better (this is where your input matters)

The platform encodes the **signed Ways of Working** - so "how it should be used" is the
standard we agreed, not an open question. But your real-world use is what refines it:

- **Rate Jane's answers** (the feedback buttons) when a priority or recommendation is off.
- **Flag a rule that fired wrongly or is missing** via **Management > Playbook Controls**
  (propose a change; it's reviewed before anything activates).
- **Keep your CRM data clean** - the automation is only as good as the HubSpot data
  (owners, renewal dates, contact roles). The **Management > Data Gaps** page shows
  exactly what's missing on your accounts.

If something feels wrong or missing, that's a signal worth raising - the platform is
designed to be tuned by the people using it, within the one standardised way of working.
